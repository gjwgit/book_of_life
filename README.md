# Your Book of Life

The book of life was conceived by the chief of the U.S. National
Office of Vital Statistics, Dr. Halbert L. Dunn 1946 together with the
term record linkage in a talk given in Canada in 1946.

This simple Flutter-based app will use Solid PODs to store an
individual's data privately and securely.

The current app itself serves as a simple demonstration of quickly
pulling together an app using the SolidScaffold() widget from the
solidui pacakge.
